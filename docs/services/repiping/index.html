Repiping in Murfreesboro and Middle Tennessee

Repiping means replacing the supply pipes that bring water through your home. It is a bigger job than a single repair, and it is usually considered when pipes keep failing or are in poor condition. D&Sons Plumbing can look at your plumbing and talk through whether repairing or replacing makes sense.

A repiping conversation generally starts with a look at the existing pipes and the problems you have had. From there you can decide whether a repair is enough or whether replacing a section, or more, is the better step. Replacing pipes involves opening access to the lines, installing the new piping, reconnecting fixtures and checking everything for leaks.

If you are only having trouble in one spot, a repair may be all you need. If you are seeing problems in several places, it is a good reason to have the whole system looked at.

It helps to have a few details ready when you call or text: where in the house the problem is, when you first noticed it, and whether it is getting worse. If water is involved, tell us whether you have been able to shut it off. You do not need to know the cause or use plumbing terms. A plain description of what you see, hear or smell is enough to get started, and it makes it easier to work out what needs to be done before anyone arrives at your door.
